# Fabrication Instructions

This folder contains all the parts required to build the robot. You will need to **3D-print** several components and **laser-cut** others, based on the details below.

---

## 3D-Printed Parts

- **Material**: Use a tough plastic such as **ABS**.  
- **Infill**: Print with **80% infill** for optimal strength.  
- **Alternative Filaments**: Stronger filaments (e.g., PETG, Nylon, or Carbon Fiber-reinforced) are optional. ABS is sufficient if cost is a concern or you already have it on hand.

---

## Laser-Cut Parts

- **Material Thickness**: All laser-cut parts should be **5 mm** thick.  
- **Material Options**: Acrylic, plywood, MDF, or aluminum.  
- **Recommendations**:  
  - **MDF** and **acrylic** often provide a good balance of cost, availability, and performance.  
  - If weight or rigidity is critical, consider aluminum.  
  - Plywood can also be used for a more natural aesthetic.

---

## Additional Notes

1. **Check CAD Files**: Ensure you have the correct dimensions and tolerances from the design files before fabrication.  
2. **Assembly**: After printing and cutting, verify all parts fit correctly before final assembly.  
3. **Safety**: Follow best practices when using 3D printers, laser cutters, and other fabrication tools.
